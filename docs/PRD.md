# ExecuMate AI — Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Product Vision
ExecuMate AI is designed to reduce manual administrative and analytical work by unifying business reporting, AI-assisted research, document intelligence, and task management into a single, high-performance web platform.

## 2. Problem Statement
Executive leadership, directors, and operations managers struggle with:
- Fragmented spreadsheets and manual KPI compilation.
- Time-consuming document reading and regulatory reviews.
- Lack of centralized tracking for cross-functional deadlines.
- Repetitive data cleansing and report formatting.

## 3. Core Modules
- **Module 01: Executive Dashboard**: Real-time portfolio KPIs, velocity charts, and high-priority alerts.
- **Module 02: AI Research Assistant**: PyMuPDF-extracted text, RAG vector retrieval, executive summaries, and grounded Q&A with verifiable page citations.
- **Module 03: MIS & Data Analytics**: Automated dirty dataset ingestion, null imputation, deduplication, dynamic KPI calculation, and downloadable formatted Excel spreadsheets.
- **Module 04: Task & Project Management**: Milestone tracking, Kanban/Table views, priority tiers, and overdue deadline monitoring.
- **Module 05: Automation Center**: n8n webhook orchestration, cron-scheduled reporting, and PostgreSQL audit logging.
