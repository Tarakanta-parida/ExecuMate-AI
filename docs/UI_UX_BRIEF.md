# ExecuMate AI — UI/UX Design Brief

## 1. Design Direction
- **Style**: Minimal, light, professional, executive productivity SaaS.
- **Avoid**: Dark mode-only defaults, neon gradients, complex futuristic animations, and cluttered layouts.
- **Focus**: High data density, clear visual hierarchy, accessible typography, and intuitive workflows.

## 2. Color Palette
- **Primary Accent**: `#2563EB` (Royal Blue)
- **Background**: `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50)
- **Card Surface**: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
- **Primary Text**: `#1E293B` (Slate 800)
- **Secondary Text**: `#64748B` (Slate 500)
- **Border Gray**: `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200)
- **Success Green**: `#16A34A` (Emerald 600)
- **Warning Amber**: `#D97706` (Amber 600)
- **Critical Red**: `#DC2626` (Red 600)

## 3. Typography
- **Font Family**: Inter, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, Segoe UI, Roboto
- **Main Heading**: 24–28px, Bold
- **Section Heading**: 16–18px, Semi-bold
- **Body & Controls**: 13–14px, Regular & Medium
- **Tables & Badges**: 11–12px, Semi-bold / Monospace

## 4. Business UX Language Guidelines
- Instead of *"Execute ETL transformation"*, use **"Clean my data"**.
- Instead of *"Invoke LLM endpoint"*, use **"Ask AI"**.
- Instead of *"Generate artifact payload"*, use **"Create report"**.
- Instead of *"Ingestion job status: 200 OK"*, use **"Document indexed successfully"**.
